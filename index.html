About

About Us

We are a specialized consulting firm dedicated to bridging the healthcare gap between Asia and Africa. We provide expert medical training, facilitate financing for essential medical equipment and devices, and support the registration of medical devices in Kenya.

Africa Asia Health Dynamics Limited is the Kenyan subsidiary of AA Health Dynamics Inc. (Tokyo), with our team based in Nairobi.

Our Mission

Resolving local medical issues by empowering local medical workers.

Our Vision

Continuing to take on emerging countries as a business development professional.
